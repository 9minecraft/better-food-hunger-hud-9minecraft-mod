# Better Food & Hunger HUD 9minecraft Mod

Better Food & Hunger HUD 9minecraft Mod adds clear visual indicators for food stats, saturation, and exhaustion directly to your screen. It shows how much hunger and saturation each meal restores when you hover over items in your inventory. When holding food, the mod previews restored health and hunger shanks before you take a bite. This mod keeps vanilla mechanics untouched while giving you full visibility over hidden hunger values.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **squeek502**'s original work, kept running on current releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Loader: Fabric, fabric, forge, NeoForge, neoforge.
